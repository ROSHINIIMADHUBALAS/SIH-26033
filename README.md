## Problem Title
**SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' earnings and increase consumer prices.**

## Problem Creator's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description

In India, farmers work hard to grow vegetables, fruits and other agricultural products. However, their earnings are often reduced because their products pass through several middlemen before reaching consumers. Each intermediary adds an extra cost, which increases the final price of the products.

Small farmers also face difficulties in finding customers, transporting their products and knowing the actual market price.

There is a need for a simple system that connects farmers directly with buyers and makes selling agricultural products easier and more profitable.

## Proposed Solution

**Project Name: Uzhavan Direct – From Farmers to Consumers**

Uzhavan Direct is a digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers, shops, restaurants and wholesale buyers.

Farmers can register on the platform, upload details of their available products, mention their quantity and set their expected selling price. Buyers can view the available products and place orders directly with farmers.

The platform will also include a common delivery system where nearby farmers can combine their products and share transportation costs.

A demand prediction feature can help farmers understand which products are likely to have higher demand based on previous sales and market trends.

This makes the selling process easier for farmers while helping consumers purchase fresh agricultural products at reasonable prices.

## Technical Approach

The proposed platform can be developed as a simple web or mobile application.

**Technologies to be used:**
- Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
- Backend: Python.
- Database: MySQL.
- Demand Prediction: Basic data analysis using Python.
- Location Support: Maps integration for finding nearby buyers and delivery locations.

**Working Process:**

1. Farmers register and create their profiles.
2. They upload available agricultural products with quantity and price.
3. Buyers browse the products and place orders.
4. Farmers receive order details and confirm availability.
5. Nearby orders are grouped to reduce transportation costs.
6. Products are delivered to the buyers.
7. Sales information is recorded to help estimate future demand.

## Feasibility and Viability

This project is practical because it can be developed using commonly available technologies and does not require expensive equipment.

Farmers can access the platform through smartphones, while buyers can use the same platform to purchase products.

Some farmers may not be familiar with digital applications, so the platform should have a simple design with local language support.

Delivery arrangements and product availability may also create challenges. These can be managed by connecting nearby farmers and buyers and updating product availability regularly.

The project can first be tested with a small group of farmers and local buyers before expanding to other areas.

## Impact and Benefits

- Farmers can receive better prices by reducing the number of intermediaries.
- Consumers can purchase fresh products at reasonable prices.
- Small farmers get an opportunity to reach more customers.
- Shared transportation can reduce delivery expenses.
- Better demand information can help reduce unnecessary production and food wastage.
- Direct communication between farmers and buyers can improve trust.

Overall, this platform can support farmers financially and make agricultural products more accessible to consumers.
